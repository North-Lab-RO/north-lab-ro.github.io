## From headlines to stories

Four stages turn a stream of headlines into a handful of stories worth reading. Scroll through them below.

<CounselorStory lang="en" />

Under the hood, the work runs as queued jobs on four separate queues, so bulk article processing never blocks the
high-value story analysis. Two models split the load: a small one handles every article, and a larger one runs only
where many sources meet.

ever reached was 65.6, so fixed 75/50/25 lines would have painted everything grey.
